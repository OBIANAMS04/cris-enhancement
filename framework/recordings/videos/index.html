CRIS rehearsal videos

Crash Records Information System, a Colaberry rehearsal case study on synthetic data. Nothing is connected to TxDOT.

Narrated by OBI with co-host Oge in synthetic Windows voices (Microsoft David for OBI, Microsoft Zira for Oge). The line being said runs as a footnote along the bottom, and the screen zooms in on what it names. Every click in the live parts is real, in the workspace running locally. The videos are WebM (VP9 + Opus), which Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Firefox play directly; press play, or open the file link to watch full screen.
