## The script (about 325 words: about 2:30 at 130 words a minute, leaving room to breathe inside 3:00)

**Slide 1 (0:00 to 0:15).** "Ali, the Crash Records update. The build is done and on track for the twenty-fourth.
The case-study recordings are behind: they were due on the thirtieth of October, and [n] of the six are up."

**Slide 2, what is done (0:15 to 1:15).** "What is done. All sixteen stories are verified by the portal: fifty of
fifty acceptance criteria, eight hundred points. The evidence I'd point to is the rehearsal: one browser walk that runs
all sixteen stories in the order I'll demo them, from an empty workspace, and passes. Seventy-three unit tests pass
too. Demo prep is finished: the run-of-show script, the rehearsal, and the demo-day presentation. Rehearsing found
two real problems, a refusal message drawn off screen and a doubled word, and both are fixed. For the recordings:
the Working-System Demo and the Project Introduction come first, as you asked, recorded locally with the server
running; the kits for the other four are ready."

**Slide 3, what is blocked (1:15 to 1:55).** "What is blocked. The evaluation step scores vendor responses with
weights we proposed: technical thirty-five, experience fifteen, key personnel ten, security and delivery ten, price
thirty, built from public Texas procurement examples, because the solicitation's real criteria and weights have not
reached us. That is waiting on TxDOT, through you. Six more answers are open with them: Euna access, TxDOT's risk
matrix, the required reports, financial-system access, legal review of the compliance register, and the service
levels."

**Slide 4, the proposed next action (1:55 to 2:40).** "My recommendation: on Demo Day, show the evaluation with the
proposed weights, labelled on screen as proposals, which the page already does. The scoring rules work the same with
any weights: weighted scores, a score far from the others flagged, a correction that keeps the original. So the demo
proves the method. When TxDOT sends the real weights, they replace one file and nothing else changes."

**Slide 5, the decision (2:40 to 3:00).** "So one decision. For Demo Day: option A, show the evaluation with the
proposed weights, labelled as proposals. Option B, leave the evaluation step out until TxDOT answers. I recommend A.
Which one?"

Then stop talking.
