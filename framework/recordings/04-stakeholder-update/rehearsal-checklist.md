# Stakeholder update: rehearsal checklist (PREP-4)

## The portal's PREP-4 hand-in

PREP-4's hand-in is **one real rehearsal in front of one real person**: give the whole demo (slides and live system)
to a classmate, colleague or friend, then ask them three questions and write down the answers:

1. What was the problem?
2. What did it do?
3. What would stop it going wrong?

Hand in on the PREP-4 Practice stage: who you rehearsed with, and what they told you, plus one thing you will change.
Only you can do this part; nobody should write it for you.

## The portal's checklist (stakeholder update)

- [ ] My first sentence says what changed, not what I worked on.
- [ ] My evidence is a number or an artefact, not an adjective.
- [ ] My blocker names a specific person, system or answer I am waiting on.
- [ ] I end with one question, not three.
- [ ] I come with a recommendation, not just a problem.

**The drill:** read the script, then delete every sentence that does not change what Ali would do. Deliver what
survives. Time it: it must land inside 3:00 without rushing the question.

## Five likely questions, with answers from the build

1. **"Why were the recordings late?"** The build came first, and I put the sixteen stories ahead of the recordings.
   That was the wrong order for the deadline; the kits for all six now exist and the videos go in before the 24th.
2. **"Are the proposed weights defensible?"** They are built from public Texas best-value procurement examples, with
   price at 30%, and the page labels them a proposal. No CRIS-specific weights are public.
3. **"What happens in a live engagement?"** The client's values replace `content/procurement-rules.json` and
   `content/solicitation.json`; the scoring, flagging and archive work unchanged, and the unit tests check them.
4. **"Is anything else at risk for the 24th?"** The demo machine: the morning-of checklist covers it (Central time
   zone, four sample PDFs, a fresh demo store), and there is a backup video and the screenshots. The demo
   solicitation's closing time is 4 December, so submissions work on the day.
5. **"What do you need from me besides the decision?"** Nothing else. The slot on the 24th is whichever Colaberry
   assigns.
