# Stakeholder update: runbook (PREP-4)

There is no live demo in a stakeholder update: the slides carry the evidence. This runbook is the setup and the
backup.

## Starting state

1. Open `framework/recordings/04-stakeholder-update/presentation.html` (double-click it in File Explorer).
2. Fill in the number: on the portal (Projects → Demo prep) note how many of the six recordings are complete. Say it
   on slide 1 in place of `[n]`. Do not edit the slide; say it.
3. Press **F** for full screen and **T** to start the timer when you begin. Amber at 2:30, red at 3:00.
4. Hide notifications (Focus / Do not disturb), close other tabs.

## Safe data

Nothing private is shown: the slides hold the portal's totals, the test counts and the proposed weights, all of which
are already in the public repo.

## If asked to show the evidence (questions, not the 3 minutes)

| Evidence | Where |
|---|---|
| 16 of 16 stories, 800 points | The portal (Projects → the CRIS build), or `framework/PROJECT-FRAMEWORK.md` section 3 |
| The rehearsal passing | `tests/demo-rehearsal.js` and its screenshots in `framework/screens/demo/` |
| The proposed weights, labelled | The workspace Evaluation page (`node scripts/serve.mjs`, then http://localhost:4321/workspace/#/evaluation) |

## Backup

If the slides will not open, deliver it from `speaker-notes.md`: it is written to be said without slides.
