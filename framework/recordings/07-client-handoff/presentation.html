Client handoff · CRIS · 5 / 6

When it misbehaves

"The chain is broken"

A stored audit line was changed.

Download the audit log first; it names the line. Do not edit it back by hand.

A response is refused

Working as designed: the message names what is missing.

It is logged as an incident until put right.

A document will not store

Over 10 MB, a refused file type, or a role without access.

The message says which.
