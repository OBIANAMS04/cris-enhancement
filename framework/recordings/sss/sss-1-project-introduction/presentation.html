Who it helps

A Colaberry rehearsal case study on synthetic data. Not connected to the real Selective Service System.

Citizens

Register, check an exemption, apply, and see the reason for every decision.

Case managers

One queue, automatic checks, a second reviewer when a check fails.

Administrators

Give and remove access, with a reason, in the app.

Every action goes in a write-once audit log.

Leadership

Totals and trends. No personal data.

The cost, the human, the next work

Proof it runs

46 stories in 4 releases, all addressed. 1,737 tests passed from a fresh clone.

A cost you can point to

Running cost is now zero. The hosted site is retired, and it runs locally.

The database cost $6 a month; the last charge was $1.12.

What people still decide

Every exemption, denial and override is a person's decision. The assistant never decides eligibility.
