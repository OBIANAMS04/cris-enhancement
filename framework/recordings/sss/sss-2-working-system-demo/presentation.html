One outcome, end to end, including the failure

A Colaberry rehearsal case study on synthetic data. Not connected to the real Selective Service System.

The outcome

A citizen registers, is checked for an exemption, and gets a decision with a reason.

The failure

A decision made without its evidence is caught and sent to a second case manager.

How it runs

Screens from the working build, captured on synthetic data. The hosted site is retired; it runs locally.

What it protects

The number is masked, with a Show control.

Password: 12 characters, an uppercase letter, a digit and a special character.

The number is stored as a one-way hash, never logged.

The first screen the citizen meets already says what this is.

Step 3: when the answer is no

A denied case, with its reason

Reason on the timeline: proof of age was not provided.

Refused, not silent

A denial needs a reason; the case manager cannot skip it.

The citizen sees the reason and can appeal.

The timeline: submitted, in review, denied, each with who and when.

Step 4: a decision that fails its checks

Compliance review open

Approval failed two controls: no assigned reviewer, no proof of age on file.

A second person resolves it

Not the person who decided.

A written justification, kept in the audit trail.

Accept the exception, or reopen the case.

Eight controls are checked on every decision.
