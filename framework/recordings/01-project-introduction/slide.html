CRIS Enhancement · Project introduction

A response missing one document is caught, explained and tracked

Obi Anamelechi Kingsley
TxDOT Crash Records Information System (CRIS)
Service Agreement solicitation
Who
A TxDOT procurement officer
receiving vendor responses on closing day. Must catch the one missing a required document, and show why it was turned away.
What changes
Every response is checked before it is stored.
All documents, on time→Stored with a fingerprinted receipt
One missing→Refused with the reason→Logged→Tracked to resubmission
The cost of a miss
One missing file = the whole response is never scored
Late responses are not considered at all. Source: TxDOT published terms (RFO 601440000054109 sec. 17.1, 17.6; Terms and Conditions 2.02(b)).
What people still decide
Every score, every flagged score, and the award
The system does the arithmetic. TxDOT alone makes the award.
