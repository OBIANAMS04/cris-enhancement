## Who is this for?

TxDOT procurement officers and contract managers who receive and evaluate vendor responses to the Crash Records
Information System (CRIS) Service Agreement solicitation. They need every response checked for the closing time and
every required document before it is stored, and a recorded reason when one is turned away. The vendors benefit too:
an incomplete response is refused with the reason while there is still time to resubmit, instead of being found
non-responsive later.

This 90-second introduction is for Ali Muwwakkil and Ram Katamaraja at Colaberry, and for an interviewer who has
never heard of the project.

## What do you want to happen afterwards?

1. Ali and Ram accept the CRIS rehearsal as complete, with its working assumptions (scoring weights, required
   documents, closing time, service levels) labelled on screen and kept in one file each, since TxDOT is not consulted
   in a rehearsal.
2. Ali and Ram confirm that the 24 November Demo Day runs on the response-submission moment: a complete response
   recorded with its receipt, an incomplete one refused, logged and resubmitted, ending on the audit log showing "The
   chain is intact".
3. An interviewer who has never heard of the project can say, in one sentence, who it helps (a TxDOT procurement
   officer), what a miss costs (a response without every required document is never scored), and what people still
   decide (every score and the award).
